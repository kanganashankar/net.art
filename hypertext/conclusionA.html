Personality House

The Way Out

The portal carries you back to the front doors, and they
swing open on their own. As you step outside, something small
is pressed into your hand: a folded letter with your name on it.

Holding it fills you with a strange sense of familiarity,
like meeting someone you've always known. You unfold it and read:

You are Type A

You notice the details other people miss. You like to understand
something before you act on it, and you rarely leave things unfinished.

  • Organized and prepared
  • Careful and observant
  • Driven to finish what you start
  • Likes a plan and a clear sense of direction
  • Can be hard on yourself when things don't go as planned

The house was never testing you. It was just showing you how you move through the world.

Walk back to the doors and begin again
