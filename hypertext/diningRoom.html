The Dining Room

You step through the door and it closes behind you.
It seems the humming was coming from a long dining room.
The table stretches across the room, covered in an intricate
assortment of food that seems to be freshly made. However,
there is no one in sight.

Underneath the clocks rests a small key on top of a table.
You wonder if it opens up another door in the room.

What do you do?

Look around to see who left the food.

Eat the food.
