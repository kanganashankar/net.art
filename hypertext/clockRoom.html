Personality House

The Room of Clocks

You step through the door and it closes behind you.
You are overwhelmed by the sound of ticking within the room.
There are 20 different clocks hanging on the wall. You observe
that most of them show the same time, except for one.

Underneath the clocks rests a small key on top of a table.
You wonder if it opens up another door in the room.

What do you do?

Examine the clocks to figure out why one of them is different from the others.

Your curiosity gets the better of you, and you take the key to unlock a door.
