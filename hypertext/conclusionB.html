Personality House

The Way Out

The portal carries you back to the front doors, and they swing open
on their own. As you step outside, something small is pressed into your
hand: a folded letter with your name on it.

Holding it fills you with a strange sense of familiarity, like
meeting someone you've always known. You unfold it and read:

You are Type B

You follow your curiosity and trust that things will work out.
You'd rather explore something than plan it.

  • Relaxed and easygoing
  • Curious and spontaneous
  • Creative and open to new things
  • Adapts easily when plans change
  • Can lose track of time or leave things unfinished

The house was never testing you. It was just showing you how you move through the world.

Walk back to the doors and begin again
