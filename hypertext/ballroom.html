Personality House

The Ballroom

While examining the clocks, you remove the one odd
clock off the wall to get a closer look. As you hold
and observe it, you find a small button on the back.
You press the button.
Suddenly, the room starts rumbling, and the wall opens
up to reveal a secret doorway.

You walk through the doorway and find yourself in a
grand ballroom. A voice echoes through the room:
"The dance begins when you are ready."

What do you do?

Join the dance floor immediately. You don't want to keep anyone waiting.

Stay at a table for a while and listen to the music before you join. There is no rush.
